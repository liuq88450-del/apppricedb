What the price tag hides

  • Yotpo is not one price: each product line is priced separately, so a store using reviews and loyalty pays two subscriptions.
  • Its 'free plan' is capped by monthly order volume (50), which means a growing store crosses into paid without changing anything.

How to read this

Start from the billing unit, not the headline price. If your store sits above the entry tier, the number that matters is the next tier up, which is why we publish the unit rather than a single "starting at" figure.
