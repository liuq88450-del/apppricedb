Loyalty and rewards. Billed by monthly order volume (the Classic base price covers 500 orders/month).

What the price tag hides

  • Only Classic has a published price. The page asks for your monthly order volume first and uses it to price and recommend a plan, so the number you see moves with store size.
  • Advanced and Plus are both 'talk to sales' plans. There is no published rate card for either, which means the jump from $199 depends on your order volume and how you negotiate.
  • The volume slider runs from 500 to 1M+ orders a month, so the $199 entry point only applies to the smallest band.

How to read this

Start from the billing unit, not the headline price. If your store sits above the entry tier, the number that matters is the next tier up, which is why we publish the unit rather than a single "starting at" figure.
