Klaviyo pricing: what you actually pay

Email & SMS marketing. Billed by active profiles (contacts who can receive marketing email).

Every figure below was read from the vendor's own pricing page on 2026-11-06. Nothing here is inferred.

Plans

PlanPriceWhat is included
Free$0250 active profiles, 500 email sends/month, $5 of SMS/MMS credit/month
Emailfrom $20/mo500 active profiles, expanded email send capacity
Email + SMSfrom $35/mo500 active profiles, email plus SMS/MMS credits

What the price tag hides

  • Every plan is priced by contact count, not by seats: above the entry tier the price rises with active profiles and is shown only in Klaviyo's own pricing calculator.
  • Exceeding the free plan's 250 profiles or 500 sends switches the account to a paid plan, it does not just throttle sending.

How to read this

Start from the billing unit, not the headline price. If your store sits above the entry tier, the number that matters is the next tier up, which is why we publish the unit rather than a single "starting at" figure.

Found an error? Send the vendor page showing the right figure. Our own mistakes are logged in the corrections log.
