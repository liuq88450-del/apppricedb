Recharge pricing: what you actually pay

Subscriptions. Billed by monthly plan fee plus a percentage and a fixed fee on every transaction.

Every figure below was read from the vendor's own pricing page on 2026-11-27. Nothing here is inferred.

Customvolume-based ratesHigh-volume subscription brands; contact sales

What the price tag hides

  • The $149 is the cheapest part. At 1,000 subscription orders a month with a $60 average order, the processing fee alone is 1.50% of $60,000 ($900) plus 1,000 x $0.20 ($200), so the real monthly bill is about $1,249, not $149.
  • The percentage means the bill rises with revenue, not with usage: a good month costs more than a quiet one on the same plan.
  • SMS is metered separately at +$0.03 per segment in the US and Canada and +$0.06 internationally.
  • No processing rate is shown for the Scale tier in the pricing block, so its all-in cost cannot be computed from the page.

How to read this

Start from the billing unit, not the headline price. If your store sits above the entry tier, the number that matters is the next tier up, which is why we publish the unit rather than a single "starting at" figure.
