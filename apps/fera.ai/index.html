What the price tag hides

  • Fera's free trial is temporary: there is no permanent free plan, unlike Judge.me or Klaviyo, so the $9 Startup tier is the real floor.
  • Yearly billing takes 30% off, which is the largest published annual discount among the review apps we have checked.
  • Order-volume tiers are wide (200 to 2,000 orders is a single step), so most growing stores jump from $9 to $29 in one month.

How to read this

Start from the billing unit, not the headline price. If your store sits above the entry tier, the number that matters is the next tier up, which is why we publish the unit rather than a single "starting at" figure.
