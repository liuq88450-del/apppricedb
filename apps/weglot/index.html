Translation & multilingual storefronts. Billed by translated words, shown as fixed plans.

Every figure below was read from the vendor's own pricing page on 2026-11-06. Nothing here is inferred.

What the price tag hides

  • Weglot prices in EUR and USD side by side, both excluding VAT — a store in the EU pays the VAT on top, which changes the real cost by roughly a fifth.
  • The free tier is a 14-day trial of the paid product rather than a permanent plan, unlike Klaviyo or Judge.me.

How to read this

Start from the billing unit, not the headline price. If your store sits above the entry tier, the number that matters is the next tier up, which is why we publish the unit rather than a single "starting at" figure.
