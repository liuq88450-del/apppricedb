Postscript pricing: what you actually pay

SMS & RCS marketing. Billed by flat plan fee plus a monthly minimum spend and per-message rates.

Every figure below was read from the vendor's own pricing page on 2026-11-06. Nothing here is inferred.

Plans

PlanPriceWhat is included
Starter$0/mo + $49 monthly minimum spend2 opt-in keywords, branded short-link subdomain
Growth$100/moStarter features plus unlimited opt-in keywords, live chat and email support

Postscript AI$699/moBundle of all Postscript AI products, per-message rates still apply

What the price tag hides

  • The plan fee is not the bill: messages are charged at per-message rates on top, and the Starter tier also carries a $49 monthly minimum spend.
  • A $0/mo headline plan is the one most likely to mislead a store owner comparing SMS apps by sticker price.

How to read this

Start from the billing unit, not the headline price. If your store sits above the entry tier, the number that matters is the next tier up, which is why we publish the unit rather than a single "starting at" figure.
