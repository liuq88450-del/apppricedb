Help Scout pricing: what you actually pay

Support helpdesk (incl. Shopify integration). Billed by user seats, plus paid add-ons for extra inboxes and docs sites.

Every figure below was read from the vendor's own pricing page on 2026-11-13. Nothing here is inferred.

Plans

PlanPriceWhat is included
Free$0Core shared inbox for a very small team
Standard$25 per user/moMultiple inboxes, live chat and SMS, one basic SLA, AI inbox assistant
Plus$45 per user/moAdvanced workflows, two advanced SLAs, customer portal, unlimited AI drafts
Pro$75 per user/moUnlimited workflows and SLA policies, highest scale and security tier

What the price tag hides

  • Help Scout bills per seat, not per conversation: a five-person support team on Plus pays $225/mo before any add-ons, and Gorgias at the same volume can be far cheaper or far more expensive depending on ticket count.
  • Extra inboxes cost $10/mo each and extra docs sites $20/mo each, on top of the per-seat price; annual billing takes about 16% off.

How to read this

Start from the billing unit, not the headline price. If your store sits above the entry tier, the number that matters is the next tier up, which is why we publish the unit rather than a single "starting at" figure.
