What the price tag hides

  • Judge.me prices by plan, not by order volume: a store doing 50 orders a month and one doing 5,000 pay the same $15 on the Awesome plan.
  • The free plan is not a trial; the vendor describes it as a permanent tier with unlimited review requests, so the paid tier is bought for automation and integrations rather than for volume.

How to read this

Start from the billing unit, not the headline price. If your store sits above the entry tier, the number that matters is the next tier up, which is why we publish the unit rather than a single "starting at" figure.
