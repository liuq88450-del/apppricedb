What the price tag hides

  • The plan is set by catalog size, not by searches or traffic: a store pays the same whether it gets 100 visitors or 10,000.
  • The count only includes products that are Active, assigned to the Online Store channel, and not excluded by tag. Draft or hidden products do not push you up a tier, but the moment you switch a collection live they might.
  • Annual billing saves up to 17% and the page offers a free site search audit with an annual subscription.
  • 14-day free trial with no card required.

How to read this

Start from the billing unit, not the headline price. If your store sits above the entry tier, the number that matters is the next tier up, which is why we publish the unit rather than a single "starting at" figure.
