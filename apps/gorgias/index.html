What the price tag hides

  • AI Agent interactions are billed on top of the plan: $0.90 each on annual plans, $1.00 each on monthly plans.
  • A ticket is not the same unit as an AI interaction, and each AI interaction also counts against ticket volume — a detail most pricing roundups leave out.

How to read this

Start from the billing unit, not the headline price. If your store sits above the entry tier, the number that matters is the next tier up, which is why we publish the unit rather than a single "starting at" figure.
