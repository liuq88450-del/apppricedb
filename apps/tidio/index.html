Tidio pricing: what you actually pay

Live chat and AI support. Billed by AI + human conversations per month (both draw on one pool).

Every figure below was read from the vendor's own pricing page on 2026-11-27. Nothing here is inferred.

The entry price above is only the 50-conversation case. Every quota, with AI and with human agents, is on one page: Tidio cost at every conversation quota.

What the price tag hides

  • AI-handled conversations and human conversations come out of the same monthly allowance, so switching more traffic to the bot does not buy you extra capacity.
  • The quoted numbers are the smallest conversation band. The page asks you to pick a volume, so the real price moves as your support load grows.
  • Plus AI carries usage charges on top of the $300 platform fee, which makes it the hardest plan to budget from the page alone.
  • Prices above are the annual-billing figures; monthly billing costs more for the same volume.

How to read this

Start from the billing unit, not the headline price. If your store sits above the entry tier, the number that matters is the next tier up, which is why we publish the unit rather than a single "starting at" figure.
