What the table says

  • Winner: 10 of 15 combinations go to Gorgias, 5 to Help Scout, Fera.
  • Help Scout wins wherever the team is small and the ticket load is heavy: at 3 and 5 seats from 500 tickets upward, a flat per-seat bill beats per-ticket billing.
  • Gorgias wins at low volume and large teams: at 100 tickets it costs a quarter of a three-person Help Scout bill.
  • Neither number is final: Gorgias charges AI interactions on top, and Help Scout charges extra inboxes ($10/mo) and docs sites ($20/mo).

Arithmetic is ours; inputs are the vendors'. If a vendor changes a price, this table is wrong until we update it.
