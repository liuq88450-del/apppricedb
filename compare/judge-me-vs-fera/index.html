Judge.me vs Fera: which review app is cheaper at your order volume

Judge.me charges one flat fee regardless of size. Fera charges by order volume. That combination produces a counter-intuitive answer: the flat fee wins for large stores, not small ones.

Inputs checked 2026-11-06: Judge.me Awesome at $15/mo from judge.me/pricing; Fera order tiers from fera.ai/pricing. Monthly billing; features differ and are noted below.

What this means

  • Fera is cheapest only below 200 orders a month ($9 vs $15), a very small window.
  • Between 200 and 2,000 orders Judge.me's $15 undercuts Fera's $29; above 2,000 the gap widens to $15 against $99.
  • Price is not the whole decision: Fera bundles AI review moderation and AI replies at every tier, while Judge.me's $15 plan sells automation and integrations rather than AI handling.
