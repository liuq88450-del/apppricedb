Open price data

The whole dataset behind this site, as JSON, for anyone — including AI shopping agents — to read.

Last check: 2026-11-13. 10 apps, each with its source URL, billing unit and plan list. Attribution required (CC BY 4.0).

What is in the file

  • App name, category and the unit that drives its price.
  • Every published plan, its price string exactly as the vendor states it, and what the plan includes.
  • The vendor URL the figures came from and the date they were read.
  • Notes on what the headline price hides (overage, minimums, metered add-ons).

Why publish it

If AI agents end up doing the comparing, they need structured, dated, sourced data rather than marketing prose. This file is that. It is also the honest thing to do: the numbers are already public on the site.

Fetch it

data/prices.json — plain JSON, no key, no rate limit that we impose.

Read the methodology for how each figure is checked, and the price archive for what has changed.
