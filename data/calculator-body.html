    What this calculator will not do

    • It does not compare feature sets. A cheaper app that cannot do the job costs more than an expensive one that can.
    • It uses monthly billing unless a vendor only publishes annual prices. Annual billing is cheaper at Klaviyo (ten months), Searchanise (annual is the lower of its two figures) and Upsell.com ($249 against $299).
    • It leaves out apps whose price is "contact sales" above the first paid step, and it does not guess a quote.
