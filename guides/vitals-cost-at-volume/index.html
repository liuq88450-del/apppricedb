Vitals cost at your monthly order volume

Vitals charges by order volume and gives you the same 40+ apps at every tier. Here is the bill for each band, monthly and yearly.

Prices read from vitals.app/pricing on checked date. Annual figures are simple multiplication, not a quote.

What to notice

  • Crossing 200 orders takes the bill from $29.99 to $79 — a 163% jump for roughly 1.5x the volume.
  • The feature set is identical at every tier, so the only variable is orders.
  • Because Vitals bundles 40+ apps, compare the tier price against the sum of the separate apps it replaces, not against any single app.
