How to audit a Shopify app bill: ten checks

The order matters. Each check catches a different kind of overspend, and the first three catch most of it.

Written from the billing rules in our rules library, checked 2026-11-06. This is the checklist we would run on a real bill before quoting any saving.
  1. Export the real bill, not the list price — The Shopify invoice shows app charges, usage charges and one-off purchases separately; list prices are not evidence of what you paid.
  2. Match every charge to an app and a billing type — Subscription, usage, one-off, or external billing. External ones never appear on the Shopify invoice.
  3. Find the free tiers you are paying past — Yotpo stops at 50 orders, Klaviyo at 250 profiles, Judge.me has no cap on review requests.
  4. Look for minimum spend — Postscript's $0 plan still charges $49 a month; that is not a discount, it is a floor.
  5. Check the metered add-ons — Gorgias AI interactions ($0.90-$1.00 each) and SMS messages are billed on top of the plan.
  6. Check seats against actual headcount — Help Scout is per user; an unused seat is a permanent cost.
  7. Look for overlapping apps — Two apps doing the same job is the most common waste and the easiest to fix.
  8. Compare monthly against annual billing — Upsell.com drops 17% annually, Fera 30%, Weglot about 17% on its Starter tier.
  9. Check tax treatment — Weglot publishes prices excluding VAT; an EU store pays more than the headline.
  10. Check what removing an app takes with it — Data, theme code and integrations may not export cleanly, so a cheaper bill can cost more later.

What a report should contain

  • Every app, its billing type, and the figure on the invoice.
  • Each problem with the rule it violates (for example: minimum spend, seat mismatch, overlapping function).
  • The annualised saving, with the assumption stated.
  • What is lost by changing it: data, features, integrations, migration effort.
  • A verdict: keep, right-size, consolidate, or investigate.

We do not promise a saving before seeing the bill. The checklist finds the candidates; the numbers come from the invoice.
