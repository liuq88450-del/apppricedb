Recharge cost at 100 to 5,000 orders a month: the percentage, not the plan fee

Recharge's headline is $149/mo. The part that actually sets your bill is 1.50% of the transaction value plus 20 cents per order. Here is what the two together cost at real volumes.

Read from rechargepayments.com/pricing on 2026-11-27: Starter "$149/mo", with "Processing fees at: 1.50% + 20¢ per transaction". The table is our arithmetic from those three published numbers, at a $60 average order value. Recharge also sells a Scale plan at $499/mo but its pricing page does not quote processing rates for it, so we do not guess them.

The number that matters is the last column

The plan fee is fixed, so it dominates at small volume and disappears at large volume:

  • At 100 orders you pay $259, which is 4.32% of revenue. The $149 plan fee alone is more than the percentage.
  • At 1,000 orders it is $1,249, or 2.08% of revenue.
  • At 5,000 orders it is $5,649, or 1.88% — and it never falls below about 1.83%, which is 1.50% plus the 20 cents expressed against a $60 order.

So "Recharge costs $149" is true only for a store with almost no subscriptions. A store doing 1,000 subscription orders a month pays roughly eight times the headline, and the floor of the model is the percentage, not the fee.

When the $499 plan makes sense

Scale is $499/mo, which is $350 more than Starter. If its processing rate were the same, it would never pay back. Recharge does not publish Scale's rates, so the honest answer is: ask them for the rate before assuming the bigger plan is cheaper, and compare against the table above rather than against the plan fee.

What this means for a stack

Because the fee scales with revenue, a subscription app is usually the single largest line in a Shopify app bill. In our calculator's default scenario (1,000 orders, $60 average order, plus Klaviyo, Tidio, Gorgias and Judge.me) the stack came to $1,623 a month, and Recharge was $1,249 of it — 77%.
