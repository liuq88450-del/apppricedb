Postscript cost at 1,000 to 500,000 texts a month

Postscript has no message allowance: every text costs the same per-message rate plus a carrier fee, and the plan fee buys a lower rate. Here is which plan is actually cheapest at your volume.

Plan fees and per-message rates were read from postscript.io/pricing on 2026-11-27. Carrier fee: the page states an average US rate of $0.00435 per SMS and $0.00832 per MMS, which we add on top. Assumption stated plainly: we treat the $49 Starter minimum as a floor on platform message fees, with carrier fees added after. If your invoice splits them differently the crossover points do not move, because carrier fees are identical on every plan.

Where the plans actually cross

  • Starter to Growth: 100,000 texts a month. Growth costs $100 more per month but saves $0.001 per message. $100 / $0.001 = 100,000 messages before the lower rate repays the fee.
  • Growth to Professional: 400,000 texts a month. $400 more per month at $0.001 saved per message.
  • Below ~5,400 texts the Starter minimum is dead money. 5,400 x $0.009 is $48.60, so anything under that pays the $49 floor anyway. At 1,000 texts the real cost is $53: the floor plus carrier fees, not $9.

This is the opposite of most SaaS pricing, where the bigger plan is cheaper from a low volume. Here the entry plan has no fee at all, so it stays cheapest for a long way up, and the discounts only start to matter at six-figure sending.

What the per-message rate hides

  • Carrier fees are on top of the advertised rate and are quoted as an average, so your bill varies by destination and message type.
  • MMS costs five times SMS on Starter ($0.045 against $0.009), and media RCS is $0.0087 in carrier fees alone.
  • RCS is an add-on on the Enterprise plan, not a different rate you can pick from the table.

Questions people ask

How much does Postscript cost for 10,000 texts a month?

About $133 with the Starter plan at 10,000 SMS: the $49 monthly minimum is exceeded, so it is 10,000 x $0.009 message rate plus 10,000 x $0.00435 average US carrier fee. The $100 Growth plan would cost about $223 at that volume, because its lower per-message rate only pays back above 100,000 messages a month.

When is Postscript Growth cheaper than Starter?

At 100,000 SMS a month and above. Starter charges no plan fee but $0.009 per SMS; Growth charges $100 a month and $0.008 per SMS. The $100 fee is recovered only after 100,000 messages.

What is the Postscript monthly minimum spend?

The Starter plan has a $49 monthly minimum spend. Below roughly 5,400 messages the message fees do not reach $49, so you pay $49 regardless. Carrier fees are charged on top.

Does Postscript charge carrier fees separately?

Yes. The pricing page lists average US carrier fees of $0.00435 per SMS and $0.00832 per MMS, on top of the per-message platform rate.
