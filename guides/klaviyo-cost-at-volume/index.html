What the price tag hides

  • You are billed for contacts who can be emailed, subscribed or not. Klaviyo's own help page: "Any profile, regardless of consent status, that can be emailed through Klaviyo is considered an active profile."
  • Over your contact limit, flexible overages do not help. The help page says a flex purchase "does not increase your active profile allowance" — only the send allowance. Contacts over the tier mean a forced upgrade.
  • Upgrades stick. "You will not be auto-downgraded" applies unless you turn auto-downgrade on in Billing > Preferences. A December contact spike can leave you on the higher tier in January.
  • The free plan stops instead of upgrading. Over 250 active profiles on the free plan, sending stops until you drop back under or move to a paid plan.
  • SMS is separate. Mobile messaging is priced by credits or dollar usage with a $5 monthly allowance; nothing in the table above includes it.

How to read your bill

Find your active profile count at the end of the billing cycle, not the number of subscribers you think you have. That number picks the row. Then compare the sends included in your row with what you actually sent: if you are over, the overage is charged at your current tier's unit rate, which Klaviyo itself notes can cost more than moving up a tier.

Questions people ask

How much does Klaviyo cost for 50,000 contacts?

$720 per month on the Profiles + Email plan, which includes 500,000 email sends that month. That is the step the pricing page shows for 50,000 active profiles.

How much does Klaviyo cost for 10,000 contacts?

$150 per month, including 100,000 sends. That is $15.00 per 1,000 profiles. The price per contact falls as the account grows: $40.00 per 1,000 at 500 profiles, $15.00 at 10,000, $13.03 at the top of the ladder.

What happens if I go over my contact limit?

You are moved up a tier: Klaviyo upgrades the plan at the end of the billing cycle to one that covers your active profile count, and it does not automatically move you back down. Flexible overages only add send capacity, not profile capacity.

Is the price per email sent, or per contact?

Per active profile. Email send capacity is included in each step; you only pay overage if you send more than the step allows. Klaviyo counts a profile as active whether or not that person has subscribed.
