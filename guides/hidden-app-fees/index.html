The hidden fees in Shopify app pricing

Every charge that does not appear in the headline price, collected in one table. All 10 apps we track, checked 2026-11-13.

Each row comes from the vendor's own pricing page, read on 2026-11-13. Where a vendor publishes nothing, the row says so.

The five shapes of a hidden fee

  • Volume bands — the feature set never changes, the price does (Vitals, Fera).
  • Overage — you go past the included amount and pay per unit (Gorgias tickets).
  • Metered add-ons — AI interactions, SMS messages, extra inboxes (Gorgias, Postscript, Help Scout).
  • Minimums — a free or cheap plan that still requires a floor spend (Postscript's $49).
  • Tax and billing period — VAT excluded (Weglot), annual discounts that vanish on monthly billing (Upsell.com).
