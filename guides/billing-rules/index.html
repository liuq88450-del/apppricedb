Rules read from vendor pricing pages and checked 2026-11-08. 21 rules across 10 apps. Also available as JSON: data/rules.json.

Why rules beat prices

  • A price changes occasionally; a rule decides what happens every month. Knowing that Gorgias AI interactions consume ticket volume explains a bill no price table can.
  • Rules cross-reference: a seat-billed helpdesk plus a volume-billed review app produce a bill that moves in two directions at once.
  • This is the table we use when auditing a real bill, and the reason it is published rather than kept private: verification is the product.
