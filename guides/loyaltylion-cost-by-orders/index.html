LoyaltyLion cost by order volume: $199 floor, then $50 per 500 orders

LoyaltyLion's Classic plan is a straight line: $199 a month covering 500 orders, then $50 for every additional 500 orders up to 4,000. Above that it becomes a quote. Because the ladder is linear, the cost per order falls the whole way up, and a small store pays a lot per order.

The eight price points were read from the price table embedded in loyaltylion.com/pricing on 2026-11-27 (the page's own selector data: PRICES = 500:$199, 1000:$249, 1500:$299, 2000:$349, 2500:$399, 3000:$449, 3500:$499, 4000:$549). The page's order selector offers bands above 4,000 but publishes no price for them; the code sets those to "Custom", so we do not guess. Advanced and Plus are quote-only.

What the shape tells you

  • The $199 is a floor, not a price for small stores. A store doing 200 orders a month pays the same $199 as one doing 500. At 500 orders that is $398 per 1,000 orders ($0.40 an order); at 100 orders the same $199 works out to $1,990 per 1,000 orders, because the plan does not scale down.
  • Every 500 orders costs exactly $50 more. That is $0.10 per order in the marginal range, and it is the same at 1,000 orders as at 4,000. There is no volume discount inside the ladder — only the fixed $199 gets diluted.
  • Per-1,000 cost falls from $398 to $137 between the first and last published band. That is the whole benefit of growing on this plan.
  • The ceiling is a quote, not a price. At 4,500 orders the page switches to "Custom". If you are near that line, ask before you plan a budget around it.

How this compares to being quoted

Because the ladder is linear and predictable, LoyaltyLion is one of the few apps where you can budget a year ahead without a quote: pick your band, add 8% if you expect growth of about 800 orders a month. The moment you cross 4,000, that predictability ends — which is worth knowing before you build a loyalty programme around the platform.
