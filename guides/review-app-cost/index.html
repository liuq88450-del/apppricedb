What Shopify review apps cost at your order volume

Four review apps, priced on the paid tier, compared by monthly order volume. One answer dominates almost every row, and it is not the one most "best review apps" lists give.

Inputs checked 2026-11-13: Judge.me Awesome $15/mo (flat), Fera tiers $9/$29/$99, Vitals order bands $29.99/$79/$179/$299, Yotpo paid reviews "from $15/mo" with volume-based scaling the vendor does not publish. Monthly billing, no add-ons.

What this table says

  • Judge.me's flat $15 is the cheapest paid tier at every volume above 200 orders, because the others raise the price as the store grows and it does not.
  • Fera only wins below 200 orders, where its $9 Startup tier undercuts everyone.
  • Yotpo's entry price looks competitive, but its real cost is volume-scaled and not published; a store has to talk to sales to find out.
  • "Best review app" lists rank on features and relationships, not on what the bill becomes at 5,000 orders. That is the gap this page fills.

Features differ: Fera bundles AI moderation and AI replies at every tier, Vitals bundles 40+ apps, Yotpo bundles loyalty as a separate line. Cheapest is not the same as best fit.
