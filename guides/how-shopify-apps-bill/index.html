The billing models behind Shopify app prices

Two apps with the same headline price can bill you completely differently. Every app we have verified, grouped by what actually drives the number.

All nine apps below were checked on 2026-11-06. The grouping is ours; the billing units are quoted from each vendor's pricing page.

Per ticket

  • Gorgias — support tickets per month

Per seat

  • Help Scout — user seats, plus paid add-ons for extra inboxes and docs sites

Per order volume

  • Vitals — monthly orders in the store
  • Upsell.com (formerly ReConvert) — order volume, with plan names tied to store size

Per contact

  • Klaviyo — active profiles (contacts who can receive marketing email)

Flat fee

  • Judge.me — flat monthly fee (not order or review volume)

Flat fee plus metered messages

  • Postscript — flat plan fee plus a monthly minimum spend and per-message rates

Per word / plan

  • Weglot — translated words, shown as fixed plans

Product line plus volume

  • Yotpo — product line plus monthly order volume

Why this matters more than the price

  • Per-seat pricing punishes team growth; per-ticket pricing punishes campaign spikes. A quiet month and a Black Friday month can cost wildly different amounts on the same plan.
  • Flat-fee apps are the only ones where your bill is predictable, which is why Judge.me's $15 and Postscript's $699 are easier to budget than a per-ticket helpdesk.
  • Metered add-ons are where budgets break: Postscript charges per message on top of the plan, Gorgias charges per AI interaction, Klaviyo moves you to a higher tier when contacts grow.
  • Yotpo is the warning case: one vendor, several product lines, each billed separately. A store can be on two Yotpo plans at once without realising it.
