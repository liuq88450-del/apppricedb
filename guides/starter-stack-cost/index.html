What a five-app Shopify stack costs

Five apps a typical store runs, each on its cheapest sensible plan, with every price read from the vendor's own page. Two store profiles, two totals.

Prices checked 2026-11-13: Klaviyo Email $20 (500 profiles), Judge.me $15 flat, Gorgias Starter $10 / Basic $60, Vitals $29.99 (under 200 orders), Weglot Starter $17. No add-ons, no annual discount.

What moves the total

  • The two totals are $91.99 and $141.99; the entire $50.00 difference is one helpdesk tier, nothing else changed.
  • Klaviyo is the only line that will rise without anyone installing anything: the $20 tier stops at 500 profiles.
  • Weglot's $17 is the USD list price; EU customers add VAT on top.
  • These are entry tiers. A store at 5,000 orders and 5,000 profiles pays substantially more, and the exact figures depend on tiers the vendors do not publish in a machine-readable way.

We only include apps whose prices we have verified from the vendor page. That is why this stack has five apps rather than the eight or ten some guides list.
