Which Shopify apps get more expensive as your store grows

Nine of the ten apps we track charge more as the store grows. One does not. Knowing which is which is the difference between a predictable bill and a surprise.

Billing mechanics from each vendor's pricing page, checked by 2026-11-13. The classification is ours; the mechanisms are theirs.

Why it matters

  • A store's app bill is not fixed at install time. Most of these lines move without anyone changing a plan.
  • Judge.me is the only flat bill in our set, which is why it wins the review-app comparison at every volume above 200 orders.
  • The dangerous pairs are metered plus volume: Postscript charges per message and has a minimum spend, Gorgias charges AI interactions that also consume tickets.
  • Before a growth push, check the next band for every app on the rise. A Black Friday month can put a store into a higher tier for a full cycle.
