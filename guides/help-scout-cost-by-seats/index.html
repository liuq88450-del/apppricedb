Help Scout cost by team size

Help Scout bills per seat. Extra inboxes ($10/mo) and docs sites ($20/mo) sit on top, and annual billing takes about 16% off.

Per-seat prices from helpscout.com/pricing. Multiplication is ours.
Team sizeStandardPlusPro

10 seats$250$450$750
20 seats$500$900$1,500

What to notice

  • A five-person team on Plus costs $225 a month before add-ons — the same money buys a lot of tickets on Gorgias.
  • Seat-based pricing punishes hiring; ticket-based pricing punishes campaign spikes. Which risk matters depends on the business.
  • The free tier covers one seat, so it is a trial in practice rather than a small-team plan.
