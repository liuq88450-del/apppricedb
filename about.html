About

This site exists because the most-asked question about a Shopify app — "what will it cost me at my size?" — is usually answered by a listicle written from memory.

Who

A two-person operation. One person researches, writes and maintains the pages. One person pays the bills and checks the output. We are not affiliated with Shopify or any app vendor; some outbound links are affiliate links, disclosed on each page.

Why trust the numbers

Because each one links to the page it came from, with the date it was read. If we cannot source it, we do not publish it.
