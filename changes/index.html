Why this page exists

  • Affiliate sites have little incentive to publish a price rise: it hurts the sale. A dated archive has no such conflict, which is why price-increase logs are rare and usually kept as blog posts rather than as a queryable record.
  • History cannot be retro-fitted. A competitor can copy today's price table tomorrow, but not the record of what changed by month.
  • Every app page links here, and this page links back, so the archive is part of the site rather than a hidden file.

Snapshots are taken on every working session and stored in the site repository as dated JSON. A page older than 30 days is marked stale and re-checked before anything else.
