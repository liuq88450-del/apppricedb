How these numbers are checked

A pricing claim without a date is a rumour. Every table on this site carries a check date and a source link.

What we record

  1. The plan names and the advertised monthly and annual price.
  2. The unit that changes the price: contacts, review count, tickets, orders, seats.
  3. What the free plan excludes, and where the first paid tier starts.
  4. Add-ons charged outside the plan price, if the vendor publishes them.

What we do not do

  • We do not install or trial the software. We do not claim hands-on experience we do not have.
  • We do not fill a gap with a plausible number. Unpublished means "not published".
  • We do not rank by commission. Commercial relationships are disclosed on each page.

Update cycle

Every app page is re-checked at least once a quarter. Price changes found in between are logged in the changelog the week they are found.

Corrections

If a figure is wrong, send the vendor page that shows the right one. Corrections are applied and logged with the date.
